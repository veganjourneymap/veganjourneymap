## Eating your way through a new city, one plant-based meal at a time

A new city is best tasted slowly. Skip the crowded main square at noon and look for the small places where locals actually eat: a bakery with a short menu, a market stall with a queue of regulars, a café that writes its specials on a chalkboard. Ask what is seasonal and what can be made without dairy or eggs — most kitchens are happy to adapt when you ask with a smile. Carry a few snacks for the gaps between meals, especially on travel days, and keep water close. In the evening, choose one restaurant and linger: order several small plates, share them, and let the staff suggest something you would not have picked yourself. If a place is fully booked, take it as a recommendation and return earlier the next day. Note the meals you loved, with the street and the dish, so you can find them again or tell a friend. Good [vegan travel guides](https://veganjourneymap.com/) turn this kind of wandering into a habit, so every trip leaves you with new flavours and a longer list of places to return to.
